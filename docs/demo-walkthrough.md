# What We Know — prototype walkthrough

## Core demonstration

An established product designer opens a specific challenge. A newer designer from another team contributes a method and an alternative. An accessibility specialist contributes a relevant prior evaluation; an engineer explains the implementation constraints. The owner uses the exchange to revise the work, and the contributor chooses whether to connect by name.

The demonstration separates age, job role and company tenure. Nobody is assigned a fixed teacher or learner role. Optional anonymity is a participation hypothesis, not a demonstrated improvement in psychological safety.

## Other working features

- Named and anonymous replies in the original conversations.
- An anonymous reply hides the profile and disables attaching a named shared experience, which could reveal identity.
- A stable alias continues the laboratory discussion across follow-up messages.
- Identity reveal requires confirmation and applies only to that discussion, including earlier anonymous contributions there.
- Revealing identity does not change contributions in other discussions; each discussion uses a separate alias. Wording and context can still identify someone.
- Once identity has been revealed in a discussion, the composer does not promise anonymity there again.
- **My project** distinguishes Maya’s own portal project from Alex’s challenge she is contributing to.
- Samir’s earlier queue-evaluation account is linked from the challenge and available in Shared experiences.
- **What changed** credits the anonymous alias until the contributor chooses to reveal their name.
- Navigation A and B can be opened and compared; the revised review includes C. Queue filters, sample details and return navigation work.

## What this implementation establishes

The prototype demonstrates the interaction sequence and a prepared case study. The dated owner follow-up and outcome are authored scenario stages, not automated replies to arbitrary input. The draft is written for that case. It does not evaluate what a visitor types.

All contributions, invitations and identity choices remain in this browser. No live colleagues are contacted. Company deployment would require an identity and permissions model, clear anonymity and moderation rules, and a shared backend. Do not describe the current interface as guaranteeing anonymous participation or as evidence of better learning, productivity or retention.

The outcome is a revised prototype and evaluation plan. It does not claim completed operator testing or improved task performance.
